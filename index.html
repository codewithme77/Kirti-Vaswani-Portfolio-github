Problem Statement

Senior product candidates spend significant time searching fragmented job sources and manually deciding whether each role fits their skills, seniority, location and work preferences.

User Persona

Senior, Principal, Group and Director-level product candidates looking for relevant roles without repeating the same search and comparison work across multiple boards.

Infrastructure Used

Lovable-hosted interface, FastAPI backend on Render, parallel aggregation across JSearch, Arbeitnow and RemoteOK, PyMuPDF resume parsing and RapidFuzz weighted matching.

Skills & Tools

Product framing, journey design, API orchestration, resume parsing, ranking logic, full-stack prototyping, deployment and iterative testing.

Problems It Solves

  • Consolidates multiple job sources into one flow.
  • Extracts relevant skills from a resume.
  • Ranks roles by role, domain, location, experience and work-model fit.
  • Filters weak matches so users can focus on stronger opportunities.

Product Approach

A transparent, deterministic scoring system was chosen for the current MVP so users can understand the dimensions behind each match. The product is an AI-assisted build and an intelligent matching engine, not an LLM-based recommender.

Problem Statement

Young intermediate players need a safe, structured way to practise badminton fundamentals without depending on expensive coaching, an account or continuous internet access.

User Persona

Young intermediate badminton players, with parents, guardians and coaches as supporting users.

Infrastructure Used

Responsive HTML, CSS and JavaScript, GitHub Pages delivery, device-local progress and a downloadable offline package.

Skills & Tools

Learning-journey design, progressive disclosure, quiz feedback, gamification, offline product design, responsive UX and responsible youth sports content.

Problems It Solves

  • Turns an unstructured goal into a five-day progression.
  • Combines practical drills with corrective quizzes.
  • Makes progress visible through milestones and a growing trophy.
  • Works without an account and can run offline.

Product Approach

One small skill per screen keeps cognitive load low. Local progress and an offline package remove connectivity and privacy barriers, while simple milestones reinforce completion.

Leadership Case Studies

Where ownership shows

Selected enterprise initiatives framed around context, accountability, decisions, execution and measurable result.

Context

Global customer operations relied on high-volume email and chat workflows spanning languages, teams and service centres. AI experiments needed to become dependable operating products.

My Role

Owned CRM platform strategy, investment roadmap, P&L business cases and cross-functional execution across product, engineering, data science, operations and customer service.

Challenge

Increase adoption without sacrificing output quality, safety or operational control across complex global workflows.

Approach

  • Scaled LLM-enabled chat and email resolution.
  • Launched AI voice, translation and intelligent routing workflows.
  • Defined an AI product dashboard for autonomy, precision and task completion.
  • Implemented LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation for quality, safety and correctness.

Result

Scaled across 45M cases, increased AI adoption from 6% to 17%, onboarded 5k agents and enabled the migration of 8M cases to lower-cost centres.

Key Learning

Enterprise AI adoption accelerates when workflow value, operational change and quality measurement are designed as one product system.

Adoption is not a launch metric. It is the result of trust, workflow fit and a disciplined feedback loop.

Context

Billing and collections workflows lacked end-to-end transparency, leaving customers and service teams with fragmented views of invoice status and outstanding balances.

My Role

Led the product from business case through roadmap, prioritisation and delivery, aligning commercial, finance, service, data and engineering stakeholders.

Challenge

Create one Salesforce-integrated workflow that could connect post-order billing, invoice visibility and collections without disrupting critical revenue operations.

Approach

  • Mapped the end-to-end customer and agent workflow.
  • Prioritised invoice transparency and actionable collections steps.
  • Sequenced delivery around revenue risk and operational readiness.
  • Connected the platform to the broader CRM experience.

Result

Built the platform from 0→1 and enabled recovery of $35M+ in outstanding customer revenue.

Key Learning

In enterprise transformation, the highest-leverage product boundary often follows the money flow rather than the organisational chart.

Own the full outcome, not only the interface where the problem becomes visible.

Context

JioMeet was expanding across consumer and enterprise segments and needed a product model that could translate platform adoption into repeatable commercial value.

My Role

As Lead Product Manager, owned 0→1 strategy, growth, go-to-market and monetization while leading and mentoring three product managers.

Challenge

Balance frictionless acquisition with differentiated enterprise packaging, activation and support efficiency.

Approach

  • Designed subscription-led monetization and commercial packaging.
  • Used segmentation and freemium conversion to expand pipeline.
  • Improved onboarding and early activation through product analytics.
  • Introduced self-service workflows for high-friction journeys.

Result

Delivered 15% revenue uplift, grew adoption 30%, expanded enterprise pipeline 25% and reduced support demand 8%.

Key Learning

Growth and monetization reinforce each other when packaging is designed around differentiated user value rather than feature volume.

Scale comes from aligning the user’s first value moment with the business model that can sustain it.

Context

Pay-as-you-go cloud services depended on converting infrastructure usage into accurate financial transactions and transparent customer invoices.

My Role

Owned 0→1 product strategy and delivery for the usage-based monetization engine, financial integrations and live billing experience.

Challenge

Join technical telemetry, pricing logic, SAP ERP workflows and customer-facing transparency into one dependable product system.

Approach

  • Defined the telemetry pipeline for compute and storage usage.
  • Shaped rating, pricing and recurring revenue calculations.
  • Integrated financial workflows with SAP ERP.
  • Launched live usage, spend forecasting and invoice visibility in GreenLake Central.

Result

Increased platform adoption 15% and reduced invoice dispute queries 37% through transparent, self-service billing.

Key Learning

Billing is not back-office plumbing; in a consumption product, it is a core customer experience.

When customers can see and understand value consumption, trust becomes a product capability.
